How Norfolk Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
